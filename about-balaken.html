About Balakan

Balakan is a beautiful region located in the northwestern part of Azerbaijan, near the border with Georgia and Russia. Surrounded by the majestic Caucasus Mountains, Balakan is well known for its breathtaking nature, rivers, and green forests.

The region is rich in history and culture, with many traditional villages where people preserve their customs and way of life. Balakan is also famous for its friendly hospitality, making it a must-visit destination for travelers.

Visitors can enjoy mountain hikes, river picnics, and cultural experiences in the heart of Balakan. Whether you are a nature lover, a history enthusiast, or just looking for a peaceful getaway, Balakan has something special to offer.
